03 — The System

Four layers, one closed loop

L1 · DIAGNOSE
BANTAY-LUPA
the space layer — “land watch”
Sentinel-1 PS-InSAR · 6-day revisit · mm-precision · GNSS-anchored
Free radar satellites map exactly where and how fast the ground is sinking — municipal-wide, every quarter, at zero image cost.
L2 · MEASURE
BANTAY-TUBIG
the ground layer — “water watch”
~40 LoRa nodes · 6 monitoring wells · 2–3 gateways + public network
Solar-powered piezometers, flow meters and salinity sensors give the aquifer a set of vital signs, streamed over LoRaWAN.
L3 · PREDICT
UTAK-TUBIG
the AI layer — “water mind”
MODFLOW 6 · RF/XGBoost · LSTM · 100% open-source
Physics + machine learning fuse both layers into a defensible, barangay-level Extraction Budget — how much pumping is safe.
L4 · TREAT
SIBOL
the action layer — “new growth”
2 injection wells · infiltration basin · 8 rainwater retrofits
Managed aquifer recharge, rainwater harvesting and community water banking put water back — and the loop closes.
↻ Then back to orbit. Every peso of action is re-measured by Sentinel-1 — space verifies what the ground reports, and the loop repeats until the sinking slows.
